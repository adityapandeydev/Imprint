# Imprint

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
Discover books, inspect their published editions, and maintain a personal reading collection.

## Users
Readers organizing what they want to read, are reading, have finished, or have put aside.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Existing React and TypeScript frontend, Go API, and PostgreSQL persistence. Preserve search, edition selection, ratings, notes, tags, Goodreads import, exports, reading goals, public profiles, and privacy controls. This redesign changes the frontend only; backend repairs and performance work are deferred by the user. Price comparison is a future capability, not a current feature.

## Brand Commitments
Preserve the Imprint name. Light mode keeps the user-selected #e5e4e2 and #e6e0f0. The user subsequently requested a genuinely dark counterpart rather than reversing the two pale colors; preserve the light appearance while adapting dark surfaces, text, controls, and accents.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing components, API contracts, tests, and real bibliographic providers. Curated discovery selections are editorial starting points, not claims about popularity or personalized recommendations. Collection statistics must come from the user's actual data.
